# Why OpenSKU

<Callout type="info" emoji="🛒">
  OpenSKU is built for the expensive decisions that happen before inventory,
  ad spend, and launch commitments.
</Callout>

Most ecommerce AI tools stop at a polished answer. OpenSKU is designed to produce work that a founder, operator, or interviewer can inspect: the evidence used, the assumptions made, the specialist hand-offs, the generated files, the verification failures, and the exact repairs that made a delivery pass.

## Evidence before confidence

Product validation often mixes public facts, calculations, and guesses. OpenSKU keeps those boundaries explicit with `observed_public`, `estimated`, and `assumption` evidence classes. A deterministic preflight checks the seven-file launch contract, source URLs, JSON/CSV structure, and unsupported no-sample claims before the pack can be delivered.

## A team, not a single oversized prompt

Launch Ultra coordinates three scoped specialists in dependency order:

- **Market Researcher** gathers market, competitor, price, and customer-language signals.
- **Offer Architect** turns the research into positioning, pricing, and validation hypotheses.
- **Asset Studio** produces listing and content assets from the approved strategy.

The Launch Director owns the final contract and repair loop. Growth Analyst is an independent top-level workflow for uploaded CSV/XLSX data, joins, cohorts, and A/B significance analysis.

## A bounded verification loop

The delivery loop is intentionally observable: act, receive a structured observation, select the smallest repair, recheck, and stop on success or budget exhaustion. Run budgets limit model calls, tokens, time, duplicate specialists, and loop iterations so a failed delivery cannot wander indefinitely.

## Reproducible by default

The repository includes bilingual recorded demos and deterministic full-stack replay tests. Replay keeps the production frontend, Gateway, authentication, uploads, LangGraph runtime, subagents, tools, checkpoints, preflight, and artifact APIs real; only the LLM provider is replaced by an input-hash-matched fixture. A separate scheduled canary covers the real-model boundary.

## Product surface and runtime

OpenSKU combines a Next.js workspace, FastAPI Gateway, LangGraph runtime, sandboxed files, cross-session memory, and a live War Room. The War Room hydrates from actual thread checkpoints so specialist and artifact state are not decorative animation.
