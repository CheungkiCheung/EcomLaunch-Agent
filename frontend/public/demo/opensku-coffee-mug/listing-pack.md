# Sample Listing Pack — Compact Travel Coffee Mug

> Draft demo copy. Every performance statement must be verified against the final physical product before publication.

## Positioning hypothesis

**A compact everyday mug for commuters who want fewer spills and less bulk—without carrying a bottle-sized tumbler.**

This is a hypothesis to test, not a validated market position.

## Working title

Compact Travel Coffee Mug for Commuting — Lightweight, Cup-Holder Friendly Design with Easy-Clean Lid

## Draft bullets

1. **Built for the commute:** a compact silhouette intended for bags, desks, and everyday travel.
2. **Simple drinking flow:** open, sip, and close with fewer steps during a busy morning.
3. **Easy-clean lid concept:** designed to reduce hidden spaces and make routine cleaning more obvious.
4. **Comfortable daily carry:** target finished weight below 350 g, subject to final engineering confirmation.
5. **A quieter visual footprint:** warm, minimal colours that work at the office, in the car, and on the move.

## Objection handling

| Likely question                 | Safe draft response                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| “Will it leak in my bag?”       | Publish only after the final product passes the documented leak protocol. Show the test rather than making an absolute claim. |
| “Will it fit my cup holder?”    | Provide the exact base diameter and a compatibility guide; avoid “fits all” language.                                         |
| “Is the lid hard to clean?”     | Demonstrate disassembly and cleaning in a short unedited video.                                                               |
| “Why not buy a larger tumbler?” | Lead with compact carry and the specific use occasion instead of claiming universal superiority.                              |

## Content angles to test

- “The five-minute station-to-desk routine” demonstration.
- Side-by-side bag-space comparison using exact dimensions.
- Unedited lid assembly and cleaning walkthrough.
- A transparent leak-test protocol with pass/fail criteria.
- “Compact or high-capacity?” audience self-selection creative.
