# Sample Evidence Ledger — Compact Travel Coffee Mug

> Deterministic demo data. Entries marked `sample_observation` are invented fixtures used to demonstrate OpenSKU's evidence contract; they are not claims about the current US market.

| ID   | Claim or input                                       | Class                | Demo status              | Required validation                                                   |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| E-01 | The target user is a commuter or frequent traveler.  | `assumption`         | Supplied by sample brief | Interview 8–12 target users and document recurring use occasions.     |
| E-02 | A compact mug should fit common vehicle cup holders. | `assumption`         | Not measured             | Define the target diameter and test representative cup holders.       |
| E-03 | Leak resistance is a primary purchase driver.        | `sample_observation` | Fixture only             | Review fresh customer language and run a structured prototype test.   |
| E-04 | A $24–$34 selling price may be acceptable.           | `estimate`           | Unvalidated range        | Test at least three price/offer combinations with the same creative.  |
| E-05 | The finished product should weigh less than 350 g.   | `assumption`         | Design constraint        | Confirm with an engineering sample and packaging specification.       |
| E-06 | Easy cleaning can reduce purchase hesitation.        | `sample_observation` | Fixture only             | Observe lid disassembly and cleaning in moderated usability sessions. |

## Evidence rules demonstrated by this sample

- A supplied brief is an input, not proof of market demand.
- A calculated or proposed range is an estimate until measured.
- Sample observations are visibly separated from live public evidence.
- Copy must not upgrade an assumption into a verified product claim.
- A launch recommendation remains conditional when physical performance is unknown.
